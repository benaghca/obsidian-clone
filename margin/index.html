How the pen works

Margin keeps one page, not a stack of notes. You write a sentence and press Enter. There is nothing to name and nowhere to file it.

While you write, the margin offers sentences you already kept, when they share uncommon words with the line under the pen. Click one and the new sentence will sit with it. That is a link, made by choosing.

Click any sentence and choose Follow to answer it or continue it. A question stays under Open until you do. Gatherings are not folders. They are the words that have come back at least three times.

What you change stays in this browser. Take the notebook as text if you want a file. Restore the sample puts the original sentences back.

Enter keeps a sentence. Shift+Enter breaks a line. / finds. Esc lets go.
